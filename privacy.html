MyAIpex – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

  • Authorization. Health data is accessed only with the owner's explicit authorization through his patient portal.
  • Storage. Data is stored only on the owner's own private computer.
  • No sharing. Data is never sold, rented, shared or disclosed, and is never used for advertising, marketing or research. There are no third parties.
  • Purpose. Only to let the owner view and understand his own health records.
  • Security. Connections to health systems use HTTPS/TLS and OAuth 2.0.
  • Deletion. The owner can delete the data at any time, and can revoke access at any time through the provider's patient portal.
  • Contact. Via the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/AdamGadway.
