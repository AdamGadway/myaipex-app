MyAIpex – Terms of Use

Last updated: October 25, 2026

MyAIpex is a personal health app built and used by one person, its owner, Adam Gadway. It is not a commercial product, is not offered to the public, and has no other users.

  1. Personal, single-user, non-commercial use. Only the owner may use MyAIpex, for personal, non-commercial purposes.
  2. Read-only access to the owner's own records. MyAIpex only reads the owner's own health records that he authorizes through his patient portal. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in any health system, and never places orders, sends messages or schedules care.
  3. No medical advice. MyAIpex organizes and displays records for the owner's personal reference. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
  4. No warranty. MyAIpex is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
  5. Revocable at any time. Access by MyAIpex can be revoked at any time through the healthcare provider's patient portal.
  6. Contact. Questions can be directed to the owner via his GitHub profile: github.com/AdamGadway.
